📑 Crisis Command Field Manual

🎯 Operational Objective

You and your team (2 to 8 commanders) constitute the Emergency Crisis Council. Your mission is to guide the metropolitan region through 4 escalating emergencies. All city statistics are authoritative and deterministic.

📊 The 4 City Vitals (Must Keep Above 0%)

  • City Integrity: Primary civic stability & structural order. If this reaches 0%, the city falls into anarchy.
  • Public Safety: Civilian survival rate, casualty mitigation, and evacuation security.
  • Infrastructure: Electrical grid, clean water conduits, transportation corridors, and telecommunications.
  • Emergency Resources: Municipal budget, fuel reserves, logistics convoys, and medical stockpiles.

🤖 NOVA AI Command System

NOVA (Networked Operations Vector Assistant) provides situational forecasts, tactical recommendations, and vulnerability telemetry. While NOVA provides intelligent tactical analysis, all scores and state changes are deterministic—they depend purely on the coordinated choices made by commanders.

⏱️ The Crisis Loop

  1. Emergency Declared: A crisis strikes with a specific threat profile draining city vitals.
  2. NOVA Tactical Briefing: Read NOVA's threat analysis and recommendations.
  3. Tactical Action Phase: Each commander has 40-45 seconds to deploy their specialized role action.
  4. Cooperative Synergies: When complementary roles act together (e.g., Evacuation Chief + Medical Lead, or Infrastructure Lead + Cyber Officer), powerful synergy bonuses are unlocked!
  5. Resolution & Survival: Survive all 4 rounds with city vitals intact to achieve victory.
